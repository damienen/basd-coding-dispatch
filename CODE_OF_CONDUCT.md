# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be direct, respectful, and specific. Technical disagreement is welcome when it is grounded in evidence and focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing unsafe instructions, fake claims, credentials, or private data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a ban from project spaces.
